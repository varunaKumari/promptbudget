# GTM Strategy — PromptBudget

## Positioning

**One-liner**: PromptBudget is a free AI spend audit tool that helps startup engineering teams find and eliminate wasted AI subscriptions — and funnels high-value leads to Credex for discounted AI credits.

**Category**: B2B lead-generation tool (not a SaaS product)

**ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)**:
- **Primary**: Engineering managers & CTOs at seed-to-Series B startups (5–50 devs)
- **Secondary**: Solo developers & freelancers managing multiple AI subscriptions
- **Qualifier**: Spending $200+/month on AI tools across the team

## Why Now?

1. **AI tool proliferation**: Most dev teams now use 3–5 AI tools (Cursor, Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, etc.)
2. **Subscription creep**: Nobody audits AI spend until the CFO asks
3. **Price confusion**: Every tool changed pricing in 2025–2026 (Cursor credits, Copilot usage-based, ChatGPT tiers)
4. **Budget pressure**: Post-2024 startups are watching burn rate closely

## Channel Strategy (First 100 Users)

### Channel 1: Indie Hacker Communities (Week 1–2)
- **Where**: X/Twitter, Indie Hackers, Hacker News, r/startups
- **What**: "I built a free tool that audits your AI spend — found $340/mo in waste on my own team"
- **Why this works**: Developers share tools that save money. The free + instant format removes friction.
- **Metric**: 50 audits, 15 email captures

### Channel 2: Dev Tool Newsletters (Week 2–3)
- **Where**: TLDR, Bytes, JavaScript Weekly, Console.dev
- **What**: "How much is your AI stack really costing?" with a link to the tool
- **Why this works**: These newsletters have 100K+ devs who are the exact ICP
- **Metric**: 100 audits, 30 email captures

### Channel 3: LinkedIn for Engineering Leaders (Week 3–4)
- **Where**: LinkedIn posts + DMs targeting CTOs, VPs of Engineering
- **What**: "We audited 50 startups' AI spend. The average team overspends by 35%. Run yours free."
- **Why this works**: LinkedIn reach for B2B is underrated. CTOs care about ROI metrics.
- **Metric**: 30 audits, 20 email captures (higher quality)

### Channel 4: Product Hunt Launch (Week 4)
- **What**: Full PH launch with "Free AI Spend Audit for Startups"
- **Why this works**: PH audience = early-adopter devs who try every new tool
- **Metric**: 200 audits, 50 email captures in launch week

## Conversion Funnel

```
Landing Page Visitor (1000)
    → Start Audit (40% = 400)
        → Complete Audit (75% = 300)
            → View Results (100% = 300)
                → Enter Email (25% = 75)
                    → Savings > $500/mo (33% = 25)
                        → Book Credex Call (20% = 5)
                            → Close Deal (40% = 2)
```

**Key metric**: 0.2% landing-to-deal conversion rate

## Credex Handoff Strategy

| Savings Level | CTA Shown | Credex Action |
|---------------|-----------|---------------|
| < $100/mo | "You're doing great" | Nurture email sequence |
| $100–$499/mo | "Save your report" | Soft pitch in follow-up email |
| ≥ $500/mo | "Book Credex Consultation" | Dedicated outbound, personalized offer |

## Distribution Hacks

1. **Shareable results**: Every audit has a unique URL with OG tags. When someone shares "$340/mo saved", their followers see it and want to run their own.
2. **Twitter/X auto-share**: One-click "Share on 𝕏" prefills a tweet with savings amount.
3. **Honest messaging for low-savings**: Telling someone "you're spending well" earns trust and referrals. They'll send it to friends who aren't.

## Competitive Moat

PromptBudget is not the product — **Credex is**. The moat is:
1. **Defensible pricing data**: Manually verified, sourced from official pages, updated weekly
2. **Audit logic**: Rules that a finance person can verify (not AI-generated BS)
3. **Lead quality**: Only high-spend teams ($500+/mo) get routed to sales
4. **Network effect**: More audits = better benchmarks = more useful tool = more audits
