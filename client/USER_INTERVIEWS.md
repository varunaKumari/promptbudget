# User Interviews — PromptBudget

> **Note**: These interviews need to be conducted with 3 real people (10–15 minutes each). The template below provides the structure. Replace the placeholder content with actual conversations.

## Interview Protocol

**Target**: Developers, engineering managers, or founders who manage AI tool subscriptions.

**Format**: 10–15 minute conversation (call, DM, or in-person).

**Key Questions**:
1. What AI tools does your team currently use? What plans are you on?
2. Who decides which AI tools to buy/upgrade?
3. Do you know how much your team spends on AI tools per month? Did you have to look it up?
4. Have you ever downgraded or canceled an AI tool? What triggered that decision?
5. If a free tool showed you exactly where you're overspending on AI, would you use it?
6. Would you share the results with your team/manager? What would make you share?

## How to Reach Interviewees

- **LinkedIn**: Search for "engineering manager" or "CTO" at startups, DM: "I'm building a free AI spend audit tool and would love 10 minutes of your time to understand how your team manages AI tool subscriptions."
- **X/Twitter**: Reply to threads about AI tools, Cursor, Copilot, etc. Ask: "Quick question — do you know off the top of your head how much your team spends on AI tools per month?"
- **Slack communities**: Post in developer Slacks (e.g., Kent C. Dodds Discord, Theo's community, etc.)
- **Friends & network**: Anyone who works at a startup with a dev team
